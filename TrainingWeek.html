Onboarding is a runway, not an information dump.

The following Monday through Wednesday builds the foundational knowledge and skills you need now. Additional solutions and advanced topics arrive as they become relevant.

Monday: Day 1

Payments, Risk & Compliance

Build the foundation for understanding how payments work, how risk shapes merchant acceptance and why compliance matters in every recommendation.

Tuesday: Day 2

Solutions, Pricing & Building a Healthy Portfolio

Connect merchant needs to the right solution, understand pricing approaches and learn how strong solutioning supports long-term portfolio health.

Wednesday: Day 3

Sales Skills & Account Retention

Practice merchant conversations, move opportunities forward and understand how the relationship continues after the signature.

Mandatory Friday Roundtable: 1 Hour

The week following training, reconnect to apply what you learned, discuss real opportunities, ask questions and identify the next skills or solutions you need.
