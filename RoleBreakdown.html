Time Management

Success at Propelr doesn't happen by accident, it happens because time gets pointed at the right things, week after week. Before you can build a plan you can actually stick to, you need an honest picture of where your hours are really going. This activity helps you build that awareness early, so you walk into the role knowing what a full week looks like, not just hoping you'll figure it out along the way.

For each category below, enter how many hours a week you think you'll spend on it. Your hours should add up to a 40-hour work week. Once you're done, select Reveal, the formula for success may surprise you.

Mission Standard

Why These Hours Matter

The Mission Standard isn't a guess. It's the weekly hour breakdown pulled directly from what actually gets new hires to quota. Every category on this page earns its place: skip prospecting and the pipeline dries up, skip follow up and deals stall, skip training and you're guessing instead of executing.

Committing to these hours from day one is what separates Pilots who graduate on schedule from those who fall behind. This isn't about working more hours, it's about pointing the hours you already have where they actually move the needle.
